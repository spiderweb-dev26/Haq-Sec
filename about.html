Who We Are

Haq Security & Cleaning Service Plc is a fully licensed and insured facility management company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We provide comprehensive security and cleaning solutions to offices, banks, embassies, construction sites, and residential homes.

Under the leadership of our General Manager, Mrs. Lamrot Kinetibeb Cherkos, we are committed to delivering professional, reliable, and accountable services. All our staff are thoroughly background-checked, professionally trained in-house, and dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of service.

Our dual-service approach allows us to offer integrated facility management solutions, providing our clients with convenience, consistency, and cost-effectiveness.
