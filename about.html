Who We Are

Haq Security & Cleaning Service Plc is a fully licensed facility management company in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We provide comprehensive security and cleaning solutions to offices, banks, embassies, and homes.

Under the leadership of General Manager Mrs. Lamrot Kinetibeb Cherkos, all our staff are thoroughly background-checked and professionally trained in-house.
