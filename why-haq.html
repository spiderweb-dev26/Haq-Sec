Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Are your staff employees or contractors?

All our staff are direct employees of Haq Security & Cleaning Service Plc. We don't use subcontractors or day laborers. This ensures consistent quality, proper training, and full accountability for every team member on your property.

Do you bring your own cleaning equipment and supplies?

Yes! We provide all necessary cleaning equipment and eco-friendly products. For specialized services or specific client preferences, we can discuss using your supplies, but our standard service includes everything needed for a thorough clean.

What happens if something is damaged during service?

We are fully insured and accountable for our work. In the rare event of damage, we have a clear reporting and resolution process. Our accountability guarantee means we take responsibility and make things right. Your property is in safe hands.

Can I customize my service schedule?

Absolutely! We understand every client has unique needs. Whether you need daily, weekly, monthly, or one-time service, we'll work with you to create a schedule that fits your requirements and budget.

Are your security guards armed or unarmed?

We offer both armed and unarmed security services depending on your needs and the nature of your property. All armed guards are specially licensed and extensively trained. We'll assess your security requirements and recommend the appropriate level of protection.

How do I get started with Haq?

It's simple! Contact us for a free consultation and quote. We'll assess your needs, discuss your requirements, and provide a customized proposal with no obligation. Once you're ready, we'll handle all the details to get started quickly and smoothly.
